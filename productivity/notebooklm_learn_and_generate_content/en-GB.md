In 2023, Google Labs launched **NotebookLM**, a note-taking and analysis tool designed to act as a personal research assistant. As someone who enjoys writing technical articles, I find it incredibly useful—it lets me pull in detailed insights from whitepapers & scientific papers I probably wouldn’t have had the time to read in full.

In this article, I explain what NotebookLM is and how to use it to learn 10 times faster, read content more quickly, and remember it better.

> The secret to learning is to gain knowledge consistently. There's no perfect age to start learning, so begin now and make this journey easier with NotebookLM.

**How it works**

NotebookLM works exclusively with the sources you provide, using a method called **RAG (Retrieval Augmented Generation)**. When you submit a prompt, RAG extracts the relevant data and feeds it to the model, which then produces a more reliable answer complete with cited sources.

To learn and memorize effectively, combine **active methods** like the Feynman Technique and the 7-3-2-1 memorization method with digital tools such as NotebookLM for best results.

### Feynman Method

The **Feynman Technique** is a powerful approach for deep understanding and memory. The process:

* Pick a concept to learn, and write its name at the top of a blank page (or a blank note on your laptop ^^)
    
* Explain the concept as simply as possible, as if teaching a child or non-expert.
    
* Review and identify gaps or confusing parts, then revisit source materials to resolve these gaps.
    
* Simplify your explanation further, using analogies and clear language.
    
* Repeat teaching and refining until the concept feels intuitive. This method works by forcing the brain to organize and clarify ideas, making memory cues stronger.
    

### 7-3-2-1 Memorization Method

The **7-3-2-1 method** is a time-based strategy that divides a study session into manageable intervals:

* 7 minutes of focused study on a single topic.
    

* 3-minute break to relax and rejuvenate.
    
* 2-minute review of the material just studied to reinforce learning.
    
* 1-minute summary, written or spoken, in your own words to consolidate memory.
    

This cycle is repeated throughout the study period. The method enhances focus, reduces cognitive overload, and incorporates active recall and spaced repetition principles for better long-term retention.

### How to Combine These Methods

* Use the Feynman technique after your review and summary: try to explain your summary aloud or to a peer.
    
* Structure study sessions using the 7-3-2-1 intervals for better pacing and engagement.
    
* After each study cycle, refine your explanations (Feynman) and solidify new knowledge.

## **NotebookLM limitations**

⚠️ **Sources limit**: Each notebook can hold up to **50 sources**, with each source limited to 50,000 characters. By consolidating this data, NotebookLM allows you to interact with your information in new ways.

⚠️ **Watch out for hallucinations:** Like any AI language model, it can make mistakes. To keep them in check:

* Use a variety of reliable sources.
    
* Approach its answers critically.
    

🔒 **Ethics & Security:**

* **Usage rights:** Make sure you’re allowed to share any data you upload.
    
* **Human voices in podcasts:** Using a real voice without permission can be illegal.
    
* **Data deletion:** You can erase your files at any time.
    

## **What’s the gain for Google in NotebookLM?**

* **Auth wall:** You can only access NotebookLM if you have a Google account.
    
* **Integrated ecosystem:** NotebookLM works seamlessly with Google Docs, Slides, and Drive, encouraging users to stay within the Google environment—potentially signing up for cloud storage subscriptions.
    
* **Gemini model validation:** It helps Google refine its AI using structured data.
    
* **A new resource for researchers:** It complements Google Scholar and strengthens Google’s presence in academia.
